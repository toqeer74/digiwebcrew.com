Apex had solid signup numbers and a weak activation rate. New accounts were onboarded by hand, and follow-up depended on whoever remembered to send the email.

## The challenge

Onboarding lived in a spreadsheet. Product usage data sat in one system, customer records in another, and nothing connected the two — so nobody could see which trials were going cold until they had already lapsed.

The team was not short of effort. They were short of signal. Customer success was spending its best hours reconstructing account state by hand instead of talking to the accounts that actually needed help.

## Our approach

We started from a simple question: at what moment does a trial become winnable or lost, and what does the team need to know at exactly that moment?

Answering it meant getting product behaviour and customer records into one place first. Automation on top of disconnected data would only have produced faster noise.

## What we built

We connected product events to the CRM so account state became visible in one place, then built a lifecycle automation layer on top.

New accounts receive a guided onboarding sequence. Usage signals trigger the right nudge at the right moment rather than on a fixed schedule. Accounts showing risk patterns are escalated to a human before the trial expires, with the context already attached.

## The outcome

Manual onboarding work dropped by roughly 70%, and trial-to-paid conversion improved 34%. The team has not missed a scheduled follow-up since the system went live — and, more usefully, now spends its time on the accounts where a conversation actually changes the outcome.
